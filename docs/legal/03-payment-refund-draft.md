## Automatic renewal

Stripe monthly and yearly subscriptions renew for another billing period unless cancelled before the next renewal. Telegram Stars subscriptions renew every 30 days unless cancelled through Telegram’s subscription settings. The applicable payment service may show its own payment and renewal notices.

## Cancellation

You may cancel a Stripe subscription through Settle’s billing page, which opens Stripe’s customer portal. You may cancel a Telegram Stars subscription through Telegram’s subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewals. You may keep using Pro until the end of the period already paid for. Cancellation alone does not result in a refund.

## Refunds

The first subscription payment is refundable on request if you contact Settle within seven days of the first charge. Later renewal payments are not refundable, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Cancelling a subscription does not refund the unused portion of the current paid period.

To request a refund or get payment help, email [settlenotes@gmail.com](mailto:settlenotes@gmail.com) and include your Telegram username or account details, the charge date, and a brief description of the issue. Do not send full card numbers or passwords. For Telegram Stars payments, Settle will review and process an approved refund through Telegram’s payment tools.
