## 1. Information Settle handles

Depending on how you use Settle, we may handle:

- Telegram account and chat identifiers and the first name associated with your account.
- Messages, captions, forwarded text, screenshots, photos, PDFs and voice notes you send to the bot, including content about other people that appears in those materials.
- Text extracted from uploads, voice transcripts, task titles and deadlines, task history, checklists, flashcards, math explanations, weak-spot topics and review prompts.
- Dashboard login and security information, including short-lived login tokens and an essential session cookie.
- Plan, subscription and payment references, billing status and support messages. Payment details entered into Stripe are handled by Stripe; Settle receives the information needed to manage your subscription, not your full card number.
- Technical and usage records used to operate, secure, troubleshoot and bill for the Service.

Do not send sensitive information or another person’s information unless it is needed and you are allowed to share it.

## 2. Why Settle uses this information

Settle uses information to provide the features you request, create and maintain your account, generate study outputs, authenticate dashboard access, manage subscriptions, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, secure the Service and meet legal obligations.

Settle does not train or fine-tune AI models on student submissions. At launch, Settle will not use submissions for secondary purposes such as internal evaluation or creating material to answer other students unless the student separately gives an optional, affirmative consent. The student can use the core Service without that consent. **This feature must remain off until the consent and de-identification process is working.**

If enabled later, Settle may use only derived study material, not raw uploads, for internal evaluation or to help answer other students. Settle will not identify or attribute that material to the student who submitted it. Settle will stop future secondary use when consent is withdrawn and remove linked retained material under the confirmed deletion process.

## 4. Retention and account closure

While your account is active, Settle keeps information for as long as needed to provide the Service and maintain your tasks, study outputs, account and subscription records.

When your account closes, Settle intends to delete raw uploads and identifiable account information within 60 days. If you separately consented to secondary use, Settle may retain only derived, de-identified study material for those purposes. To honour a withdrawal request, Settle will keep a protected link to that material until it is removed. **The maximum retention period, backup deletion schedule and time to remove material after consent withdrawal remain to be confirmed.**

The raw-file deletion, de-identification and withdrawal processes are being built. This Notice must not be published until those processes cover storage, transcripts, generated outputs, database records, backups and any derived-material collection.
